Channel playbook

How a top dealer sells Fiery

Copiers Northwest, a 12-location Pacific Northwest dealer, documented the approach that makes Fiery a reason to buy from them. Use it in dealer enablement.

What they sell

  • Four Fiery strengths: job management, color management, ease of use and processing power
  • Fiery on monochrome engines too, using presets and one interface
  • Multi-brand management, so customers run different OEMs from one place
  • G7 testing for certification and ongoing quality checks

How they sell it

  • On-site needs analysis before the sale
  • Quantify time: about 10 minutes saved per repeat job across 5,000–7,000 jobs a year, close to one full-time salary
  • Train customers to be self-sufficient with Fiery online learning
  • Stay close to Fiery engineering through monthly calls

Talk track for Americas OEM partners

  1. Open with the labor problem: training time and touches per job.
  2. Show a matching Americas proof card (iOffice, Monarch, AlphaGraphics).
  3. Start free: Command WorkStation, JobFlow Base, FreeForm Create, Fiery IQ.
  4. Upsell the paid step: JobFlow or JobFlow Pro, Impose, ColorGuard.
  5. Close with a 30-day trial and the matching ROI calculator.

Company context

Fiery at a glance

Approved boilerplate (88 words)

Fiery, LLC is a leading provider of digital front ends (DFEs) and workflow solutions for industrial and graphic arts print industries. With over 2 million DFEs sold globally, our innovative software and cloud-based technologies deliver stunning color and exceptional print quality on a broad range of production printing devices. Our customer base includes commercial print, packaging, signs and display graphics, ceramics, building materials, textiles and other specialty applications. With over 30 years of excellent support and service, Fiery has built an unmatched community of customers and partners.

Source: Fiery Brand Book, p.27. Use the longest version that fits.

Milestones that shape the story

  • 2022: acquired CADlink, now Fiery Specialty Production (Ottawa)
  • 2023: separated from EFI as an independent company
  • Dec 2024: acquired by Seiko Epson; keeps its name and structure
  • Jan 2026: completed acquisition of Inèdit Software (textile RIPs)
  • Jun 2026: Micah Kornberg named CEO, succeeding Toby Weiss
  • Pinnacle Awards: 2 in 2024, 3 in 2025, 1 in 2026

Correction to the earlier project primer

The September primer listed Siris Capital as owner and Toby Weiss as CEO. Epson has owned Fiery since December 2024, and Micah Kornberg has been CEO since June 29, 2026. The primer is now updated.
